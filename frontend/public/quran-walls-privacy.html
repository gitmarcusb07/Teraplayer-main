01 · Overview

How Quran Walls handles information

Quran Walls is an Android app for reading Quran verses and creating wallpapers. The app has no sign-in or account system. In the current release, it does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send your preferences, favorites, selected verses, or saved wallpaper recipes to a Quran Walls server.

An internet connection is required to use the app and load wallpaper artwork. The Quran text and translations used to compose wallpapers are read from the app’s local Quran database.

The app and the website have separate functions and privacy practices. This document applies only to Quran Walls in the app.

02 · Information handled

Information the app does and does not receive

Quran Walls does not ask you for personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. It does not access contacts or collect location data.

When the app requests the remote catalog or a wallpaper image, your device necessarily sends standard network information such as its IP address, the requested file path, request time, and technical connection details. The app uses those requests to deliver wallpaper content; it does not attach your favorites or other personal profile information.

The app may process verse references and text locally to render wallpapers, populate the home-screen widget, and schedule a reminder if you enable one.

03 · Online content

Wallpaper catalog and Cloudflare R2

The wallpaper catalog, thumbnails, and full-resolution artwork are hosted on Cloudflare R2. When you browse or use wallpapers, the app requests those files from the configured public host. Downloaded images may be cached in the app’s private cache so they do not need to be downloaded again immediately.

Cloudflare may process network request information, including IP address and request metadata, to deliver content and operate and protect its service. Its processing is governed by Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Quran Walls does not control Cloudflare’s infrastructure logs or their retention periods.

The app currently uses a Cloudflare R2 development host by default. The app operator may change the wallpaper host in a later release.

04 · On-device storage

What stays on your phone

The app stores information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Theme, translation, Arabic font, text layout, and other appearance settings.
  • Favorites and saved wallpaper recipes, which contain verse references and style choices.
  • Your onboarding completion and automatic wallpaper change preferences.
  • Downloaded wallpaper cache files and temporary generated wallpaper images.
  • Locally prepared daily verse data for the widget and optional reminder.

The Quran database is included with the app and is queried on the device. The app does not upload your Quran searches or verse selections.

05 · Permissions and sharing

You choose when device features are used

  • Wallpaper: When you choose Apply, Android’s wallpaper service sets the generated image on the home screen, lock screen, or both, according to your selection.
  • Gallery: The app requests permission only when you choose to save a generated wallpaper to your photo gallery. It does not need permission to browse or read your existing photos.
  • Notifications: Daily verse reminders are optional. If enabled, reminder content is scheduled on the device and displayed by Android. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in Android settings.
  • Widget: If you add the Quran Walls widget, verse content and its display preferences are stored locally so Android can update the widget.
  • Sharing: If you share a wallpaper, Android opens the system share sheet. The app passes the image to the destination you select; that destination’s privacy practices then apply.

06 · Service providers

Third-party services involved

Cloudflare R2 hosts and delivers the wallpaper catalog and artwork. Android provides system features such as notifications, wallpaper setting, gallery saving, and sharing. Google Play may process information under its own terms when you install or update the app.

The current app release does not integrate ad networks, analytics providers, or an account/authentication service.

07 · Retention and controls

Managing or removing app data

Locally stored app settings and saved wallpaper recipes remain on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall Quran Walls. Android may clear cached wallpaper files at any time; the app can download them again while online. Wallpapers you save to your gallery remain there until you delete them using your gallery app.

You can disable reminders from Quran Walls settings or Android notification settings, revoke gallery permission in Android settings, and clear app data or uninstall the app using Android’s app settings.

08 · Children

Children’s privacy

Quran Walls does not ask users to submit personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information was provided to the app operator, contact us so we can review the request.
